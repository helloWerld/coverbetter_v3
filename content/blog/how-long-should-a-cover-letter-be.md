How long should a cover letter be? For most jobs, 250 to 400 words in three or four paragraphs, which fits on less than one page. Go shorter, about 150 to 250 words, for an email or an online form. Go longer only when the employer asks for more detail, as some academic and senior roles do.

## How Long Should a Cover Letter Be for Each Situation?

The best length depends on where the letter goes and who reads it.

| Situation | Word count (body) | Paragraphs |
|---|---|---|
| Standard application with an uploaded letter | 250 to 400 | 3 to 4 |
| Online form with a text box | 150 to 300 | 3 |
| Cover letter in an email | 150 to 250 | 2 to 3 |
| Entry-level or internship | 200 to 300 | 3 |
| Senior or executive role | 300 to 450 | 4 to 5 |
| Career change | 300 to 400 | 4 |
| Academic or research role | Often 1 to 2 pages; follow the post | Varies |
| Internal transfer | 150 to 250 | 2 to 3 |

If the job post gives a limit, follow it. Some posts ask for "a short note" or "a few sentences about why you want this role". That means 100 to 150 words, not a full letter.

If you draft with [CoverBetter](/), you can choose the length to match the situation. The options are Short (about 200 words), Standard (about 300 words), and Detailed (about 400 words).

## Why Shorter Usually Works Better

A hiring manager often reads many applications for one role. A short letter is easier to read in full. Each extra paragraph is one more chance for the reader to stop before your best point.

A short letter also forces you to choose. When you can only include two results, you pick the two that match the job best. That choice is the main skill a good cover letter shows.

Too short is also a problem. A letter of three sentences with no example tells the reader nothing new. The goal is the shortest letter that still gives real proof.

## Length of Each Part

Once you know how long a cover letter should be, use this table as a budget when you write.

| Part | Sentences | Words |
|---|---|---|
| Greeting | 1 line | 2 to 5 |
| Opening paragraph | 2 to 3 | 40 to 70 |
| Body paragraph 1 | 3 to 5 | 60 to 110 |
| Body paragraph 2 (optional) | 2 to 4 | 50 to 90 |
| Closing paragraph | 2 to 3 | 30 to 60 |
| Sign-off | 2 lines | 3 to 6 |

If one part goes over budget, look there first when you cut.

## Example: A 250-Word Cover Letter

This example shows how much fits in about 250 words. Replace the items in square brackets.

> Dear [Hiring Manager Name],
>
> I am applying for the [Job Title] role at [Company]. For [number] years I have [main type of work], and your post's focus on [requirement from the post] matches the work I do best.
>
> At [Current Company], I [action you took] to fix [problem]. Within [time period], [measurable or clear outcome]. I did this by [one or two specific steps], and I worked with [team or partners] to keep it going. Your team wants to [goal from the job post], and I would bring the same approach to that work.
>
> I have also [second relevant result in one or two sentences]. This gives me a practical base for [another part of the new job].
>
> I would welcome the chance to talk about how I can help [Company] with [goal]. Thank you for your time and consideration.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your Name]

When you fill in the brackets with real details, this template lands between 200 and 280 words. For the full method to write each part, read our guide on [how to write a cover letter](/blog/how-to-write-a-cover-letter).

## Page Length and Layout

Your cover letter should fit on one page with normal settings: 1-inch margins, a font size of 10.5 to 12 points, and single spacing. If you need smaller margins or a tiny font to fit, the letter is too long. Our [cover letter format](/blog/cover-letter-format) guide lists the full settings.

A letter of 250 to 400 words on one page leaves white space. White space is good. It makes the letter easy to read.

## How to Cut a Long Cover Letter

Most first drafts are too long. Use these steps to cut a draft down to size.

1. Delete the first sentence if it says "I am writing to apply for...". Start with the second sentence.
2. Remove any sentence that repeats a line from your resume with no new context.
3. Keep only the two results that match the job's top requirements. Save the others for the interview.
4. Cut general claims such as "I am a hard-working team player." Keep claims with proof.
5. Combine short sentences that say the same thing.
6. Remove filler phrases: "I believe that", "I feel that", "in order to", "the fact that".
7. Shorten the close to two sentences.
8. Read it again. If a paragraph has more than five sentences, split it or cut it.

### Before and after

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "I am writing to express my strong interest in the open position of Marketing Coordinator at your company." | "I am applying for the Marketing Coordinator role at [Company]." |
| "I believe that I would be a great fit for this role due to the fact that I have experience in social media." | "I ran social media for a local retailer for two years and grew our email list by running monthly giveaways." |
| "I would be very grateful if you would consider my application and I look forward to hopefully hearing from you soon." | "Thank you for your time. I would welcome a conversation about the role." |

## How to Lengthen a Short Cover Letter

If your letter is under 150 words for a standard application, it probably lacks proof. Add one of these:

- One specific result with a number or a clear outcome
- One sentence on why you want this company, based on something real (a product, a project, a value they state)
- One sentence that explains a career change or a gap, if your resume raises the question

Do not add length with general praise for the company or a list of soft skills.

## Character Limits in Online Forms

In an online form, the answer to how long should a cover letter be often comes from the form itself. Some forms limit the cover letter box to a number of characters, not words. A rough guide is that one word in English averages about five to six characters with the space. So:

| Character limit | Approximate words |
|---|---|
| 1,000 | 150 to 180 |
| 2,000 | 300 to 370 |
| 3,000 | 450 to 550 |

Write in a document first, check the character count, then paste it. If the form cuts your text, the reader sees a letter that stops in the middle of a sentence.

## Quick Length Check

- Body is 250 to 400 words for a standard letter
- 150 to 250 words for an email or a short form
- Three or four paragraphs
- No paragraph over five sentences
- Fits on one page with 1-inch margins and an 11-point font
- Every paragraph has a clear job: open, prove, or close
